Privacy Policy — Secure Nest

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Secure Nest is built privacy-first. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information. If anything here is unclear, please contact us.

The short version: Your photos, videos, audio, documents, filenames and album names never leave your device. They are encrypted on your iPhone with a key we do not have.

Your vault stays on your device

The files you add to Secure Nest and the names of your albums and items are stored only on your iPhone. They are encrypted before being written to disk. We cannot read them because the encryption key is generated on your device and never sent to any server.

How encryption works

  • Every file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, an industry standard used by banks and governments.
  • A unique 256-bit encryption key is generated the first time you sign in and stored in the Apple Secure Enclave / iOS Keychain. It is tied to this device and never leaves it.
  • Filenames, album names and your profile information are also encrypted before being saved to local storage.
  • Your 4-digit PIN protects access to the app. Face ID or Touch ID can unlock the vault as a shortcut; your biometric data stays on your device and is never accessible to us.

What we DO collect

We use Firebase (Google) for sign-in. The only personal data stored on our servers is:

  • Your email address — used for login and password reset.
  • Your password — stored in hashed form by Firebase. We never see it in plain text.
  • Basic sign-in events (time, device type) used only to detect abuse.

Firebase Crashlytics sends us anonymous crash reports (function name, line number, device model, iOS version) when the app stops unexpectedly. Firebase Analytics records basic usage events (screens visited, feature counts, session duration). Neither of these contain content from your vault.

What we DO NOT collect

  • We do NOT upload your photos, videos, audio, or files.
  • We do NOT see your filenames or album names.
  • We do NOT share or sell any data to third parties.
  • We do NOT run ads inside Secure Nest.
  • We do NOT track you across other apps or websites.

Device permissions we ask for

  • Photo Library — to import photos and videos into your vault.
  • Camera / Microphone — if you record directly into Secure Nest.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — to unlock the vault faster.
  • Files — to import documents.

Each permission is used only for the stated purpose.

PIN security and auto-wipe

For your protection, after too many wrong PIN attempts the app temporarily locks itself. The wait time increases at 5, 10, 15, and 20 failed attempts. After 23 wrong attempts spread across multiple 24-hour lockouts, the vault is automatically erased from the device to prevent brute-force attacks.

If you forget your PIN, tap "Forgot PIN?" on the lock screen to recover by confirming your account password instead. You will then be able to set a new PIN without losing your data.

Deleting your account

Delete Account inside Settings will:

  • Remove all encrypted files from this device.
  • Delete the encryption key — making any backups unreadable.
  • Delete your Firebase account (email + login info).

This is permanent and cannot be undone.

Children

Secure Nest is not intended for users under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country, whichever is higher).

Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy, you will be notified in-app before the change takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will also change.
